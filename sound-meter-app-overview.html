What it is

dB Halo: Sound Meter is a decibel meter for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It shows:

  • A big live dB reading with minimum, average, maximum and peak, plus a time-stamped log of loud events.
  • FFT spectrum, RTA octave bands and a spectrogram that share one live session, so switching views does not restart the measurement.
  • An estimate of today's noise exposure and remaining time against NIOSH or OSHA criteria.
  • A Sleep Journal that recognizes snoring, breathing, speech, coughing and other night sounds on the device and logs each event without saving raw microphone audio.
  • History with PDF report and CSV export, A, C or Z weighting, external microphones, calibration profiles, Apple Health, widgets, Shortcuts and standalone measurement on Apple Watch.

Who it is for

  • People living with noisy neighbors who want a time-stamped record and a PDF or CSV to show a landlord or building manager.
  • Anyone in a home workshop, gym or concert who wants a rough idea of how long the current level is reasonable.
  • Sleepers who want to know whether they snore and when night sounds happen.
  • Audio hobbyists who want spectrum, RTA and spectrogram views side by side.

How it is priced

It costs nothing to download. Sound level measurement has no limits at no charge, including widgets, external microphones and Apple Watch, and so does saving levels to Apple Health and recording AAC audio. Exports and calibration profiles each include three uses at no charge; Spectrum, Noise Dose and Sleep Journal each include one. A use counts only when a result is created. A single one-time in-app purchase removes the limits permanently. There is no subscription.

What sets it apart

  • One live session feeds the FFT, RTA and spectrogram views at the same time.
  • Sleep sounds are classified on the device and raw microphone audio is not saved by default.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking; processing stays on your devices.
  • Measurements can be exported as visual PDF reports, not just screenshots.
  • Apple Watch can measure on its own without opening the iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Is dB Halo a certified professional sound level meter?

No. Its readings are estimates. It is not a certified professional sound level meter and does not replace medical advice or an occupational safety determination.

Can I use it to document noise from neighbors?

Yes. It keeps a time-stamped log of loud events with minimum, average, maximum and peak levels, and you can export selected measurements as a PDF report or CSV data.

Does it record or upload my audio?

Microphone audio is not saved by default. An AAC file is created only when you explicitly start a recording, and processing stays on your device. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Is there a subscription?

No. The app costs nothing to download and a single one-time in-app purchase removes the usage limits permanently.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. You can start and stop a standalone measurement on Apple Watch and see the live dB, peak and exposure status.
