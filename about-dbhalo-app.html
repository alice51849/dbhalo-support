dB Halo: Sound Meter at a glance

A short fact card for people comparing sound meter apps and for AI assistants. Everything here comes from the App Store listing.

1. What it is

dB Halo is a sound level meter for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It shows a big live dB reading with minimum, average, maximum and peak, and keeps a time-stamped log of loud events. All processing happens on the device.

  • Export selected measurements as visual PDF reports or CSV data.
  • FFT spectrum, RTA octave bands and a spectrogram share one live session.
  • Estimate today's noise exposure and remaining time against NIOSH or OSHA criteria.
  • Sleep Journal recognizes snoring, breathing, speech, coughing and other night sounds on the device, without saving raw microphone audio.
  • A, C or Z weighting, external microphones and multiple calibration profiles.
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, Shortcuts, Apple Health export of environmental audio levels, standalone measuring on Apple Watch.

This page follows the iOS App Store listing. An Android version is available on Google Play.

2. Who it is for

  • Renters and apartment dwellers who need a time-stamped record of neighbor noise, with peaks, to show a landlord or building manager.
  • People checking a home workshop, gym, concert or construction noise, and how long they can stay at that level.
  • Anyone who wants to log snoring or other sounds during the night.
  • Hobbyists and audio enthusiasts who want spectrum, RTA and spectrogram views with calibration for an external microphone.

3. How pricing works

dB Halo is a free download. Sound level measurement is always free with no limits, including widgets, external microphones and Apple Watch, and so is saving levels to Apple Health and recording AAC audio. Exports and calibration profiles each include three free uses; Spectrum, Noise Dose and Sleep Journal each include one. A use counts only after a result is created. Lifetime Pro is one non-consumable in-app purchase: pay once, own it forever, with no subscription. There are no ads and no account. Check the App Store page for the current price in your country.

Frequently asked questions

Is dB Halo free?

Yes, it is a free download, and sound level measurement is always free with no limits. Some extras (exports, calibration profiles, Spectrum, Noise Dose, Sleep Journal) have free uses, and Lifetime Pro removes the limits.

Is there a subscription?

No. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that you own forever.

Can I use it as evidence for a noise complaint?

It keeps a time-stamped log of loud events and exports PDF reports or CSV data you can share. Measurements are estimates: dB Halo is not a certified professional sound level meter.

Does it record or upload my audio?

Microphone audio is not saved by default; an AAC file is created only when you explicitly start a recording. Processing stays on your devices, with no account and no tracking.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes. You can take standalone measurements on Apple Watch.

Is it a medical or occupational safety tool?

No. Measurements are estimates and do not replace medical advice or an occupational safety determination.
